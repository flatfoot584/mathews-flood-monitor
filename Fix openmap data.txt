2. Fix the Upstream OpenStreetMap Database (Permanent Worldwide Fix)

OpenStreetMap is an open, community-edited world map (similar to Wikipedia). Anyone can update it:

Open OpenStreetMap.org
 in your web browser.
Sign up for a free account (or log in) and click the "Edit" button at the top (this launches the built-in iD web editor).
Click directly on each street:
Click on the road labeled "Little Avenue" 
→
→ In the left panel, change Name to New Little Street.
Click on the unnamed road connecting Bayshore Ave and River Rd 
→
→ In the left panel, set Name to Mobjack Street (and verify Feature Type is Minor/Unclassified Road or Residential Road).
Click on "Matthew Lane" 
→
→ In the left panel, change Name to Matthews Street.
Click "Save" in the top-right corner.
In the changeset comment box, enter something like:
"Corrected street names in Mobjack Bay Estates (New Little St, Mobjack St, Matthews St) per local signage and Mathews County E-911 records."
Click "Upload".

Once saved, the OpenStreetMap database updates immediately, and the standard OSM tile layers usually re-render and refresh within a few hours to a couple of days.
