How & When You Receive Flood Warnings

Our system is completely automated and runs every 30 minutes. It evaluates hydrological water levels and dispatches alerts along four distinct warning stages:

Stage 1: Advance Notice 6–12 Hours Ahead

Early Crest Warning

Dispatched as soon as the NOAA NWPS / CBOFS forecast first indicates that an upcoming high tide will breach the 3.99 ft flood tipping point. Gives you plenty of time to plan travel, move vehicles, or secure items before water rises.

Stage 2: Imminent Warning 1–2 Hours Ahead

Pre-Crest Hazard Alert

Dispatched 60 to 120 minutes before peak high tide. Provides specific estimated flood depths in inches (e.g., "5 to 7 inches over Daniel Ave") and states whether low passenger cars are passable.

Stage 3: Surge Escalation Immediate

Sudden Surge Increase

If persistent easterly or along-bay winds drive storm surge ≥ 0.25 ft (3+ inches) higher than the previous forecast, or if the risk tier escalates to Tier 3, an immediate update is dispatched.

Stage 4: Water Receding Post-Crest

All-Clear Bulletin

Dispatched when the Ware River gauge safely drops back below 3.99 ft, water recedes into drainage ditches, and no further high water is predicted within the next 48-hour forecast window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our privacy-first community notification system.

Why ntfy.sh instead of standard SMS text messages?

Standard SMS text messaging requires collecting and storing community members' personal phone numbers, paying telecom gateway fees, and navigating complex carrier spam filters. ntfy.sh is 100% free, decentralized, open-source, and does not require you to share any personal information whatsoever.

Do I have to create an account or give my email?

No. There are zero accounts, zero passwords, and zero email registrations. Subscribing to an ntfy topic is just like tuning a radio to a broadcast channel.

Will this wake me up at 3:00 AM?

If an extreme high tide is predicted to breach roads in the middle of the night, you will receive an Advance Warning 6 to 12 hours earlier during the daytime, giving you time to park safely before bed. Imminent crest warnings also chime so you are not trapped unexpectedly by rising water.

Will this drain my phone's battery?

No. ntfy uses standard Apple Push Notification service (APNs) on iOS and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, using virtually 0% additional battery.

How do I unsubscribe?

In the ntfy app, swipe left or long-press on mathews-flood-23128 and tap Delete. In a web browser, tap Unsubscribe in the top corner. You are instantly removed.
